Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a custom quote for bulk manufacturing? expand_more
For industrial-scale manufacturing, select "Custom Manufacturing Quote" in the contact form. Please provide your annual volume estimates and any specific tolerance requirements. Our procurement engineers will provide a formal RFQ package within 48 hours.
What are the international shipping lead times? expand_more
Standard hardware shipping to EMEA and Americas takes 5-7 business days. Consumables (filaments/resins) are dispatched within 24 hours of order confirmation. Technical parts are shipped via express courier to ensure minimal production downtime.
Do your STL licenses cover commercial redistribution? expand_more
Our standard digital asset licenses are for single-user industrial application. Commercial redistribution or mass production licensing requires an Enterprise Asset License. Contact our licensing department via the form for terms and conditions.
