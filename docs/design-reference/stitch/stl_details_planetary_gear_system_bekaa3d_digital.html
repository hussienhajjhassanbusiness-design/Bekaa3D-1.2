Engineering Details

The Planetary Gear System is designed for mechanical hobbyists and industrial prototyping. Unlike standard gear designs, this model incorporates modified involute profiles to ensure smooth rotation even when printed with consumer-grade FDM printers. The assembly is fully modular, allowing you to swap out planet gears to change reduction ratios.
