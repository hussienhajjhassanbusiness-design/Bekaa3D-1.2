From a single prototype to a global fleet.

Founded in a modest engineering workshop, Bekaa3D began with a simple mission: to make high-fidelity manufacturing accessible to the world's most ambitious designers.

Over the last decade, we have evolved into a technology-first leader, operating one of the most advanced additive manufacturing fleets in the industry. Our journey is marked by a relentless pursuit of the 0.1mm—the margin where excellence is defined.

Today, we serve Fortune 500 aerospace firms, automotive pioneers, and medical device innovators, providing the physical infrastructure for tomorrow's breakthroughs.
